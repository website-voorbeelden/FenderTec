    Yokohama fenders with tire net

    A chain and tire net surrounds the air-filled rubber body, providing an additional protective layer where the fender comes into contact with a vessel or berthing structure. The compressed air inside the fender absorbs energy during contact. This construction is used for ship-to-ship and ship-to-quay operations.

    When choosing a size, consider the required energy absorption, reaction force and available space alongside the vessel. The dimensions above help you compare the range; check our Yokohama fenders with tire net for the selected size and further product information. Our team can help assess the requirements of your application.

    Sling-type pneumatic fenders

    Sling-type pneumatic fenders use an air-filled rubber body without an external chain and tire net. The sling construction has a reinforced outer cover, with end fittings for handling and securing the fender. It provides an alternative where a pneumatic fender without netting is required.

    The right diameter and length depend on the vessel, contact conditions and required performance. Allow for the handling arrangement and check the pressure and performance data for your chosen size. Our sling-type pneumatic fenders product page provides the available size variants and further details for selection.

    Foam-filled PU fenders

    Foam-filled fenders have a foam core enclosed in a polyurethane outer skin. The foam compresses to absorb contact energy, while the outer skin protects the core against wear. Unlike pneumatic fenders, this construction does not use an inflated air chamber.

    They provide an alternative for vessel and berthing applications where a foam-filled construction is preferred. Compare the body dimensions above, then check the performance and attachment requirements for your installation. Explore our foam-filled PU fenders for the available variants, or ask our team to help select a suitable size.

    Collection: Yokohama fenders

    What are Yokohama fenders?

    Yokohama fenders are pneumatic marine fenders designed to absorb berthing energy by compressing air inside a reinforced rubber body. They are widely used to protect vessels and marine structures during berthing, mooring, and ship-to-ship operations.

    What are pneumatic fenders?

    A pneumatic fender is an inflatable rubber fender that uses compressed air as the main energy-absorbing medium. When a vessel comes alongside, the internal air pressure is compressed, allowing the fender to absorb impact energy in a controlled and progressive way. For this reason, the terms pneumatic fender and Yokohama fender are commonly used interchangeably.

    Why are pneumatic fenders called Yokohama fenders?

    The name Yokohama fender originates from the Yokohama Rubber Company, one of the first manufacturers to develop and standardise pneumatic marine fenders for global use. Over time, the term became a generic industry name describing this specific fender type and operating principle, rather than a specific brand.

    Correct spelling of Yokohama fender

    The correct spelling is Yokohama fenders. Online, this term is sometimes misspelled as yokahama fender, yokahama fenders or yokohoma fenders. These variations generally refer to the same product: a floating, air-filled pneumatic marine fender.

    Applications

    Yokohama fenders are used in a wide range of marine and offshore applications, including ship-to-ship operations, ship-to-jetty berthing in ports, offshore platforms and floating structures, dredging projects, and temporary or mobile fendering solutions where floating capability is required.
